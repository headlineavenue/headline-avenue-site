Privacy / Control

Data deletion

Disconnect a platform integration and request deletion of integration data that Headline Avenue controls.

Disconnecting a social account

You may revoke Headline Avenue's access through the connected platform's own account or app-permission settings. Revocation prevents new API access using that authorization.

Requesting deletion

Email headlineavenuemedia@gmail.com with the subject Privacy Request. Include enough information for us to identify the relevant connected account or integration. Do not send passwords or authentication codes.

What we can delete

Where applicable, we will delete personal information, stored OAuth credentials, integration metadata, and temporary media under our control, subject to legal, security, fraud-prevention, and platform obligations.

What deletion does not remove

Content already posted to a third-party platform may remain there until you delete it through that platform. Headline Avenue cannot delete data controlled solely by another service.

Verification and response

We may ask you to verify a request before acting on it. We will respond within a reasonable period consistent with applicable law and operational requirements.
