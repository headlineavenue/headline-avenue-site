Privacy Policy

How Headline Avenue handles information across our website and authorized social-platform integrations.

Effective date: September 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Headline Avenue (“Headline Avenue,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit our website or use a connected publishing integration.

1. Information we may process

  • basic profile information supplied by an authorized platform, such as an account identifier, display name, and avatar;
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens used to maintain an authorized connection;
  • video files, titles, captions, thumbnails, and related metadata you select for processing or upload;
  • technical and security information such as timestamps, request status, device or browser information, and error logs; and
  • information you send to us directly, including support or privacy requests.

2. TikTok and other platform integrations

Headline Avenue may use official third-party APIs, including TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. When you authorize an integration, we use the permissions you approve only to provide the requested connected-account functionality.

We do not sell TikTok user data or use connected-account data for unrelated advertising profiles.

3. How we use information

  • authenticate and maintain authorized account connections;
  • process and transmit user-selected media;
  • operate, troubleshoot, secure, and improve the service;
  • respond to support, privacy, access, and deletion requests; and
  • comply with applicable law and platform requirements.

4. Sharing

We may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, with third-party platforms when you direct us to use their integration, and when required by law or necessary to protect the service and its users.

5. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, subject to legal, security, and platform requirements. Temporary media and processing files may be automatically deleted according to configured retention periods.

6. Your choices

You may revoke a connected platform's authorization through that platform. You may also request access to or deletion of personal information or integration data that we control by contacting us.

7. Data deletion

Detailed instructions are available on our Data Deletion page. Revoking access prevents future use of that authorization but does not necessarily remove content already posted to a third-party platform.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Our connected-account tools are not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through these tools.

10. Third-party services

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. We are not responsible for their independent practices.

11. International processing

Depending on where services and infrastructure are located, information may be processed in jurisdictions other than where you live, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact headlineavenuemedia@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current Headline Avenue service and should be updated if our hosting, integrations, data practices, or business structure materially change.
